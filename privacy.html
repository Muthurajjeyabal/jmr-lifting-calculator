Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2026

Short version: JMR Lifting Calculator does not collect, store, transmit, or share any personal data. The app runs entirely on your device and works fully offline.

1. Who we are

JMR Lifting Calculator ("the App") is an engineering calculator suite for lifting, rigging, steel, and offshore calculations, published by JMR Lifting Solutions & Engineering.

2. Data collection

The App does not collect any personal or usage data. Specifically, the App does not:

  • Require account creation, sign-in, or any personal identification
  • Collect names, email addresses, phone numbers, or location data
  • Use analytics, tracking pixels, or advertising identifiers
  • Send any data to remote servers — all calculations run locally on your device
  • Use cookies or similar tracking technologies
  • Share, sell, or transfer any information to third parties, because none is collected

3. How the App works offline

All engineering calculations (sling tension, crane utilization, padeye checks, steel weights, and all other tools) are computed directly on your device using built-in formulas. No internet connection or external server is contacted to perform a calculation. The App is installable as a Progressive Web App and functions fully without network access after installation.

4. Permissions

The App does not request access to your camera, contacts, location, microphone, storage, or any other device permission beyond what is required to install and display the app itself.

5. Children's privacy

The App is a professional engineering tool intended for lifting, rigging, and crane industry professionals. It is not directed at children, and it does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children, because it collects no information at all.

6. Third-party services

The App does not integrate any third-party analytics, advertising, or data-processing services. If you access this policy or the App through GitHub Pages, GitHub's own hosting may log standard, anonymized web server access information (such as IP address) as part of normal web hosting; this is governed by GitHub's Privacy Statement and is outside the App's control.

7. Engineering disclaimer

This App provides preliminary engineering estimates for reference only. It does not store, transmit, or evaluate the accuracy of any values you enter — all inputs and results exist only in your device's memory during use and are cleared when you close or reload the App. Final lift plans must always be reviewed and approved by a certified Rigger or Qualified Engineer.

8. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted at this same URL with a revised "Last updated" date. Continued use of the App after a change constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.

9. Contact

For questions about this privacy policy, please contact: contact@jmrlifting.com
